Effective November 14, 2026

Pixelverse - Mr. Pixels ("Pixelverse") is an iPhone app made by John Pederson ("I", "me"). This policy says what the app does with your information.

The short version. I do not collect anything. Pixelverse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of mine. What you make stays on your iPhone. The only time anything leaves your iPhone is when you tap a button that sends a request to OpenAI or ElevenLabs, using your own account with that company.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your library: the images you generate, the rants you write, narration recordings, your characters and their descriptions, reference images, voice settings, and your generation settings.
  • Your OpenAI and ElevenLabs API keys, in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • A record that you agreed to the app's first-launch notice about what it sends.

This stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone. I cannot see it. Deleting a rant, image, reference, or character in the app deletes its files. Deleting the app deletes its library. Keys saved in the Keychain may remain on the iPhone after the app is deleted, so use Remove Key in Settings first if you want them gone.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include the app's library, under your agreement with Apple.

What is sent to OpenAI

When you tap Generate or Generate Portrait, the app sends OpenAI:

  • the scene you typed,
  • the description of the character in use,
  • that character's reference images, and
  • your OpenAI API key, to identify your account.

When you tap the access check in Settings, the app sends only your OpenAI API key.

The app comes with one reference image of its built-in character, Mr. Pixels. It is an illustration, not a photograph of a person, and it is sent to OpenAI with image requests for that character unless you remove it.

What is sent to ElevenLabs

When you record or re-record narration, the app sends ElevenLabs:

  • the text of the rant,
  • the voice ID set for the character, and
  • your ElevenLabs API key, to identify your account.

How those companies use it

These requests go directly from your iPhone to OpenAI and ElevenLabs. They do not pass through me. Each company handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy, and under the account you hold with it:

  • OpenAI privacy policy
  • ElevenLabs privacy policy

Do not put anything in a scene, a character description, a reference image, or a rant that you are not willing to send to those companies.

Photos

To add a reference image, you choose a photo with the iPhone's photo picker. The app receives only the photos you choose and cannot see the rest of your photo library. Saving a generated image to Photos happens through the share sheet and only when you choose it.

Sharing and backups

When you use the share sheet or back up your library to a folder in Files, the content goes where you send it. A backup folder holds your library but never your API keys.

Diagnostics

The app itself collects no diagnostics. If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give me crash reports that do not identify you.

Children

Pixelverse is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without sending anything by not tapping the buttons that send requests.
  • You can remove a saved key at any time with Remove Key in Settings. That deletes it from your iPhone; to cancel the key itself, revoke it on the company's website.
  • You can delete anything in your library, or all of it by deleting the app.
  • For anything already sent to OpenAI or ElevenLabs, use the controls those companies give you for your account.
